Meet the students behind the mission.

We would love to hear from students, educators, and community partners interested in practical financial literacy.

Co-Founder & Workshop Lead

Maya Chen

Maya is a high school student who started Beyond the Swipe after noticing how many everyday money questions students were expected to answer on their own. She develops welcoming workshops that make budgeting, saving, and spending feel useful instead of intimidating.

Co-Founder & Program Director

Olivia Brooks

Olivia turns student questions into practical activities and resources. She is especially interested in helping young people connect the choices they make today with future goals such as college, work, and financial independence.
