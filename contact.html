Co-Founder, Ethics & Humanities Lead

Sophia Scott

Sophia Scott is a proud co-founder of Beyond the Swipe. Sophia has always had a strong interest in finance, specifically the communication and advising side of the field. She saw the potential for a program made by youth, for youth, that could help students better understand where their money goes and, more importantly, the kind of control they have over it. Sophia leads the ethics and humanities side of Beyond the Swipe. She manages the program's social media and outreach, while also developing the writing, explanations, and student-focused content that make the program accessible and relatable.

Co-Founder & Technical Lead

Alice Zhang

Alice Zhang is a proud co-founder of Beyond the Swipe and a sophomore at Penn High School. With a longstanding interest in computer science and programming, Alice is motivated not only by what technology can do, but by how it can meaningfully improve people's lives. Her vision is to help high school students develop practical financial knowledge they can use when preparing for college, making everyday decisions, and guiding others. Alice has played a central role in shaping Beyond the Swipe from its earliest ideas, working closely with Sophia to develop the program's vision, features, and student experience. She also leads its technical implementation - from building the website to training the AI model that helps guide students. By combining thoughtful problem-solving with a strong sense of purpose, Alice helps ensure that the program's technology is accessible, useful, and grounded in the real needs of young people.
