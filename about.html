About Us

Student workshop

Beyond the Swipe is a student-centered financial literacy initiative helping teens and young adults feel more confident with everyday money decisions.

We turn topics like budgeting, saving, spending awareness, debit cards, credit basics, and financial goal setting into lessons that feel clear, practical, and connected to real life.

Our approach is welcoming and hands-on. We meet students where they are, answer the questions they may not know how to ask yet, and build habits that can support stronger choices over time.
