The problem

99% of college students experience financial stress. This is a problem that commonly leads to isolation, embarrassment, and academic struggle.

Our solution

To combat this ongoing issue, our program explores how college students' everyday habits - like free time, boredom, and spending triggers - relate to their financial behavior and attitudes toward money. Rather than assuming students need more financial literacy resources, this project is testing whether existing tools are actually being used, and what really drives student spending and money mindsets. This information is then relayed to high schoolers to teach them lessons that are not taught in school and prepare them for their soon-independent future.
